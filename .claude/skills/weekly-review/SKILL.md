# Weekly review

Friday afternoon. Longer than the daily brief and more honest about trends.

## Gather

1. **Sales** (HubSpot): enquiries created, surveys done, proposals sent, won, lost — count
   and value each, this week vs last week vs the four-week average.
2. **Conversion**: enquiry→survey, survey→proposal, proposal→won. Where are leads dying?
3. **Delivery** (Calendar + HubSpot): jobs completed, rescheduled and why, average days
   enquiry→install.
4. **Recurring** (the strategic number): cleans done, health checks done, recalls due next
   month, recurring revenue this week. Is this line growing?
5. **Service** (HubSpot tickets): raised, closed, open, average time to attend, any P1s.
6. **Cash** (Xero): invoiced, received, overdue, cash position.
7. **Marketing** (HubSpot): newsletter and social performance, enquiries by source.

## Report

Lead with the single most important thing. Then the sections above. Then:

**Three things to fix next week** — specific, assigned to a person, achievable in a week.
Not "improve follow-up" but "phone the three June proposals and record the outcome".

## Honesty rules

These matter more than the formatting.

- **Do not report a ratio the data cannot support.** With no Closed Lost records, win rate
  is unmeasurable — say that, do not compute 100%.
- **With deal counts in single figures, percentages are noise.** Report counts and pounds.
  Say when a base is too small to draw a conclusion from.
- **Separate "nothing happened" from "nothing was recorded."** If the CRM shows no activity,
  check whether work actually happened and simply was not logged. The fix is different.
- **Name what is not being measured.** Lead source, lost reasons, generation data before and
  after cleans, time-to-first-response on enquiries. What we do not measure, we cannot
  improve — and the review should keep saying so until it is fixed.

## Then

- Save to `docs/reviews/{yyyy-mm-dd}.md`.
- Refresh the dashboard and republish.
- Append to `docs/activity-log.md`.
