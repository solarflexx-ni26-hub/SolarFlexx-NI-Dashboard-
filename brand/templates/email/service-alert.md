# Template — proactive service alert

Use when monitoring has flagged a problem **before the customer noticed**. This is the most
valuable email we send. Get it right.

Tone: matter of fact. We watch these systems, we spotted something, here is what happens
next. Not apologetic — we have not done anything wrong. Not alarming — most of these are
small.

---
**Subject:** Your solar system — we've spotted something

Hi {first name},

We monitor the systems we look after, and yours has {plain description — stopped reporting /
been offline since {date} / been producing less than it should}.

{One sentence on what that usually means: "Nine times out of ten this is the wifi dongle
having lost the router rather than anything wrong with the system itself." Or, if it is
serious, say so plainly.}

{What it is costing them, if anything, and honestly: "In the meantime the system will still
be generating — it is the monitoring that is down, not the panels." Or: "While it is off you
will be buying all your electricity from the grid, so worth sorting quickly."}

What happens next: {the specific step — "Could you check whether the inverter screen is lit
and send me a photo of it?" / "I would like to get someone out to you. I have Wednesday
morning or Friday afternoon — which suits?"}

Cheers,
{Name}
SolarFlexx NI
{phone}
---

## P1 safety variant — phone first, then send this as the record

**Subject:** Your solar system — please switch off at the isolator

Hi {first name},

I have tried to ring you. Please switch the system off at the **AC isolator** — {where it
is} — and leave it off until we have been out.

{What was reported, in one sentence.}

Do not open anything up or go near the roof. We will be with you {when}.

Ring me on {phone} when you get this.

{Name}
SolarFlexx NI
