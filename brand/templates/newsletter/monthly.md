# Template — monthly newsletter

Audience: past customers and warm leads. Goal: be the name they think of. Not a sales blast.

**Subject line:** plain and useful. "Why your generation drops in November" beats
"SolarFlexx Newsletter — Issue 4". No emoji. Under 45 characters if you can.

**Preview text:** the second half of the hook, not a repeat of the subject.

---

## 1. The useful thing (this is 60% of the email)

One topic, explained properly, 150–250 words. Seasonal where possible.

Ideas by month:
- **Jan/Feb** — reading your annual figures; what a normal winter output looks like
- **Mar/Apr** — spring health check season; getting the system ready for the good months
- **May/Jun** — peak generation; making the most of a surplus; why now is the time to think
  about a battery
- **Jul/Aug** — export and self-consumption; holidays and your battery settings
- **Sep/Oct** — cleaning before the low-light months, when every watt counts
- **Nov/Dec** — why output falls off a cliff and when a drop is actually a fault

## 2. A job we did

One photo. Two or three sentences: where, what we fitted, one honest outcome. Named only
with permission.

## 3. Seasonal nudge

One service, one reason it is relevant this month, one price or "reply and I'll quote you".

## 4. One action

A single link or a single ask. "Reply to this email and I'll get you booked in" outperforms
a button, because it starts a conversation.

---

**Footer:** company name, address, phone, unsubscribe. Legally required — never remove the
unsubscribe.

**Send:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 9–10am. Never Friday afternoon, never Monday morning.

**Before sending:** every figure sourced, every claim checked, every name permitted, test
send read on a phone.
