# Social post formats

Platforms in priority order for SolarFlexx NI:
**Facebook** (where NI domestic trade actually happens) → **Instagram** (visual proof of
work) → **LinkedIn** (commercial, community and public sector) → TikTok (optional).

Every post: first line earns the scroll-stop, one clear ask, our own photos only.

## 1. Finished install

> {Place name} — {system spec in plain terms}.
> {One specific detail a normal person would notice: how tidy the cable run is, that we
> matched the flashing to the slate, that it went in between two days of rain.}
> {Ask: Thinking about it for your own place? Drop us a message.}

Photo: wide shot of the roof, plus one close-up of neat work.

## 2. Before / after clean — our best-performing format

> These panels in {place} hadn't been cleaned in {n} years.
> {What was on them: lichen, bird mess, agricultural film, salt.}
> {If you have the generation data, use it. If not, do not invent it.}
> Pole-fed, deionised water, no detergents — the way it should be done.

Photo: split before/after, same angle, same light.

## 3. Plain answer

> "{A real question a customer actually asked}"
> {Two or three sentences of straight answer from brand/faq.md.}
> Anything you want to know, ask away in the comments.

## 4. Job in progress

> {What we are doing today and where.} Scaffold up at 8, panels on by 2.
> Photo: someone actually working. Faces do well.

## 5. Community / commercial

> Delighted to have {community centre / club / farm} up and running — {spec}, {what it means
> for their bills}.
> These are the ones we are proudest of.

## 6. The team

> {Name} has been with us {time} and does {the thing they are good at}.
> People buy from people, and in NI they buy from people they recognise.

## Cadence

2–3 posts a week is plenty and sustainable. Better a real photo of a real job on Tuesday
than five stock images in one day. Batch a fortnight at a time.

## Never

Stock photos of Californian rooftops. Fake urgency. Political content. Anything about a
customer's house without their permission. Prices in a post without "from" or "typically" —
you will be held to them.
