# Customer FAQ — approved answers

Agents may use these answers directly in drafts. Anything not covered here goes to Dave.
Where an answer says `CONFIRM`, the answer is not settled — do not improvise one.

**How much does a system cost?**
Depends on roof size, orientation, your usage and whether you want a battery. Most domestic
jobs we do land between £8,500 and £12,000 with a battery. We give a firm price after a free
survey — an hour and a half, no obligation.

**How long does it take?**
Most domestic installs are two days on site. Bigger or awkward jobs can be three. Scaffold
sometimes goes up the day before.

**Do I need scaffolding?**
Usually yes for a pitched roof — it is a working-at-height requirement, not an upsell. It is
included in the quoted price unless we say otherwise.

**How much will I save?**
Depends on your usage, your tariff and how much you use during daylight. We work it out
properly at survey using your actual bills. Anyone who gives you a savings figure before
seeing your consumption is guessing.

**Will it work in Northern Irish weather?**
Yes. Panels run on daylight, not heat — they are actually more efficient when it is cool.
Output is lower in winter, which is why we size systems around the whole year rather than a
sunny week in June.

**What about when the sun isn't shining?**
You draw from the grid as normal. A battery stores the surplus from the day so you use your
own electricity in the evening, which is when most households use the most.

**How long do panels last?**
Panels typically carry a 25-year-plus performance warranty. Inverters are the part that
usually needs replacing first, commonly around the 10–15 year mark. Batteries are warranted
on years or cycles, whichever comes first.

**Do panels need cleaning?**
Yes, more than people think — especially near the coast, near trees, or on a farm. Rain does
not clear bird mess, lichen or agricultural film. We clean with pole-fed deionised water and
no detergents.

**Can I clean them myself / can my window cleaner do it?**
We would rather you did not. Jet washing and household cleaners damage the anti-reflective
coating and can void the panel warranty, and walking on a roof is how people get hurt. It is
also a working-at-height job.

**How often should they be cleaned?**
Typically every 6–12 months depending on your site. We will tell you honestly at the health
check if yours does not need it yet.

**What's an annual health check?**
A full inspection — panels, mounting, inverter, isolators, cabling, earthing, battery health
— plus a performance check against what your system should be producing, and a written
report. It catches the faults that quietly cost you money for years.

**My system has stopped working / the app shows an error.**
First: is there a red light on the inverter, and does the app show an error code? Send us a
photo of the inverter screen and the code. Do not open anything up. If there is a burning
smell, scorching, or water where it should not be, switch off at the AC isolator and ring us
straight away.

**My generation looks lower than last month.**
Usually seasonal — output drops a lot between October and February and that is normal. If it
is down against the same month last year, that is worth looking at. Send us a screenshot.
