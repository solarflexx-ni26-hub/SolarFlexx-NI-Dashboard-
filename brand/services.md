We use specialist PV cleaning equipment — pole-fed, deionised water, no detergents and no
abrasives. That matters: jet washing and household cleaners damage panel coatings and can
void manufacturer warranties. Worth saying to customers, because plenty of people will
otherwise have a go themselves or let a window cleaner at it.

### Why cleaning matters (for content and sales conversations)
Soiling reduces output. How much depends entirely on the site — coastal salt, agricultural
dust, tree cover and bird fouling are the big ones in NI, and a shaded or fouled panel can
drag a whole string down. **Do not publish a specific "cleaning increases output by X%"
figure unless it comes from a cited source or our own before/after generation data.** Our
own data is better marketing anyway — start recording generation figures either side of a
clean.

## Health check — what is actually included

Define this properly; it is what makes it sellable rather than vague:

- Visual inspection of panels, mounting, flashing and roof penetrations
- Inverter status, error log review and firmware check
- DC and AC isolator condition and operation
- Cable and connector inspection (MC4s, conduit, UV degradation)
- Earthing and bonding check
- Battery state of health and cycle data where fitted
- Generation performance against expected yield for the site
- Monitoring and app check, reconnect if dropped
- Written report with photos and any recommendations
