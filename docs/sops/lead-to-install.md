Who does what, and how fast. The AI team handles the shaded steps; a human does the rest.

## Where this currently breaks

- **Step 3** — no measurement of response time at all. Start measuring it; it is the single
  strongest predictor of winning a domestic job.
- **Step 10** — not happening. £45,245 has sat untouched for 64 days.
- **Step 12** — never happens. Zero deals have ever been marked Closed Lost.
- **Steps 16, 18, 19, 20** — not happening. These are the cheap ones, and they are what turn
  one sale into three.
